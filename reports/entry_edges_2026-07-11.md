# Part 2 — Out-of-sample validation & robustness

Method: split all settled trades at the median timestamp. Filters were defined on
half 1 (h1) and validated blind on half 2 (h2). Baseline: h1 wr=0.5039, h2 wr=0.4991 (pnl -185.01).

## Negative filters (h2 holdout)
Individually each recovers only 0.3–1.4pp and stays below break-even.
Combined (drop ADX 15-20, UTC hours {4,6,19,20,21}, macd_sign_consistency 0.5-0.8,
RSI 30-50, trend entries, bot_wr NaN/0.6-0.7): h1 0.5533 -> h2 0.5309, keeps 39%,
h2 pnl +39.89 vs -185.01 unfiltered. Above BE in both halves but p=0.28 — suggestive only.

## Positive gate: ADX>40 & entry_kind=flip  (the headline finding)
- Holdout: h1 0.5741 (n=270) -> h2 0.5620 (n=274), h2 pnl +23.48, p_vs_be=0.096
- Full sample: n=544, wr=0.5680, pnl +105.68, p_vs_be=0.0152, p_vs_50=0.0009, ~25 trades/day
- Dose-response (flip only): adx>30 53.3% | >35 53.2% | >40 56.8% | >45 58.9% (n=321) | >50 58.9% (n=185)
  Monotone above 35 — consistent with a real effect, not a lucky bin.

  USDARS 52.6% (57), MATIC 52.2% (69). Excluding best pair barely moves it (56.5%).

## Decision-point audit (skip side, last 7 days)
Skips are dominated by `weak_trade_strength: marginal_pair_wr=...` — gating on TRAILING
PAIR WIN RATE, which this project has already shown regresses to mean and does not predict.
In the last 7 days alone, ~1,400+ flip setups with ADX>40 were skipped by the pair-WR gate.
The decision logic currently weights a non-predictive signal (trailing pair WR) above the
only feature with a validated, dose-responsive edge (ADX strength on flip entries).

## Expiry-duration analysis: NOT POSSIBLE from live data
All 4,636 settled trades are 5-second expiry. Zero variation to mine. Any expiry edge
requires new data: either a small forward arm at 60s+ or offline backtests from the raw
tick streamer.

## Caveats
- Multiple comparisons: dozens of buckets were examined; the ADX gate survives holdout,
  dose-response, pair/direction/week slicing, but only forward DEMO validation settles it.
- W27 dipped below break-even (48.8%, n=80) — the edge is not uniform week to week.
- 5s OTC binaries are broker-generated prices; any edge may be repriced away.

## Recommendations (proposals only — no policy changed)
1. Forward-test an ADX gate on DEMO: only flip entries with ADX>40 (or >45 for the
   stronger/thinner variant), all other policy unchanged. ~25/day (>40) or ~15/day (>45).
   Pre-register: >=300 trades, kill if WR<52.1%.
2. Retire or heavily down-weight the marginal_pair_wr gate — it is the dominant skip
   reason and is built on a metric proven non-predictive here.
3. If expiry edges matter, create expiry variation deliberately (small 60s+ arm or
   tick-replay backtests). No amount of mining 5s-only data can answer it.
